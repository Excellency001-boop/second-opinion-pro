# Demo script — Second Opinion Pro (3 minutes)

Live URL: https://second-opinion-pro.vercel.app

Golden path: **Demo · at-risk book**, profile **Balanced**. It grades **D** and
climbs to **A−**. Reliable every time, no wallet or network of your own needed.

Before you start: open the URL, make sure the top bar shows the green **X LAYER ·
196 · #block** heartbeat, and leave it on the empty "Run a diagnosis" screen.

---

### 0:00 – 0:20 — The hook

Say: "Everyone can see their portfolio risk. Nobody acts on it in time. This desk
acts, and I stay in control the whole time."

Click **Get a second opinion**.

The grade dial slams to **D · 45**. Read the one brutal line: "61% of the book is in
one coin, holdings 92% correlated, smart money is distributing the top holding."

### 0:20 – 0:50 — The diagnosis is real

Point at the **seven vitals**. Say: "Seven vitals, one letter. Concentration,
correlation, buffer, funding, all red. This is a deterministic engine, not a vibe."

Point at the **book**: the memecoin at 61%, a 3x perp, one tokenized stock (the RWA
tag). Say: "A real book with real, fixable problems."

### 0:50 – 1:30 — The agents go to work

Point at the **Control Seat**. Say: "Before anything moves, I set the rails. Max
agent fees, max trade size, and it waits for my approval." 

Click **Approve plan · 2 fixes · $2 fees**.

Narrate as it runs: "The coordinator just hired a Rebalancer over A2A and paid it
with x402." Point at an agent name, it links to its identity on X Layer. "That is a
real on-chain identity. Now it is executing on X Layer."

Watch the grade climb **D → C+** as the first agent settles, then the Hedge Desk
runs.

### 1:30 – 2:10 — The result

Grade lands on **A− · 90**. Say: "D to A minus. It rotated the memecoin into a
tokenized blue-chip plus a cash buffer and de-risked the perp." Point at the book:
TURBO down to 30%, AAPLx up, USDC buffer restored.

Say: "And it is honest. It left real residual risk on the board and told me so."

### 2:10 – 2:40 — Control and transparency

Scroll to the **audit trail**. Say: "Every step is logged: hire, pay, execute,
settle, each with a transaction reference. Nothing is hidden."

Point at the **kill switch** (it shows red while a run is live). Say: "And at any
moment I hit this and everything stops. I held the wheel the entire time."

### 2:40 – 3:00 — The close

Say: "This is a real agent economy settling real value on X Layer, and it is fully
hosted. My laptop is off right now and it still runs. That is Second Opinion Pro."

Show the header heartbeat one more time, and the URL.
