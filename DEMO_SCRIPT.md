# Demo video — read-aloud script (about 2m30s)

How to use this: open https://second-opinion-pro.vercel.app, start a screen
recording, and read the **SAY** lines out loud while you do the **DO** actions. Keep
it calm and steady. Target length 2 to 3 minutes (the form allows 2 to 4).

Recording tips
- Record the live site in a clean browser window, full screen, nothing else visible.
- One smooth take is fine. If you fumble a line, pause and say it again, you can trim.
- Speak a little slower than feels natural. Confidence, not speed.
- Make sure the green "X Layer" dot and block number are visible in the top right.

---

### 0:00 – 0:15 · The hook
**DO:** Start on the opening screen (the "does not just warn you" page).
**SAY:**
> "Everyone can check if their crypto portfolio is risky. Almost nobody fixes it in
> time. This is Second Opinion Pro. It does not just warn you. It fixes it, and I
> stay in control the whole time. Let me show you."

### 0:15 – 0:35 · Grade the risk
**DO:** Keep "Demo · at-risk book" selected. Press **Get a second opinion**.
**SAY:**
> "It just graded this portfolio a D. Look why: sixty-one percent sits in one coin,
> the holdings all move together, and there is barely any cash buffer. In plain
> words, this book is one bad day from a wipeout."

### 0:35 – 1:05 · It is a real diagnosis, in plain language
**DO:** Move the cursor slowly across the seven **Health checks**, then the book below.
**SAY:**
> "Seven health checks, one grade, each in plain English. Leverage, biggest position,
> move-together risk, cash buffer. This is a deterministic engine, not a guess. And
> on the right, the desk has already drafted a fix, a team of specialist AI agents,
> each with a real identity on X Layer."

### 1:05 – 1:20 · I set the rails first
**DO:** Point at the **Control Seat**: the two sliders and the approval toggle.
**SAY:**
> "Before anything moves, I set the limits. How much the agents can spend, how big a
> single trade can be, and it waits for my approval. I am always in charge."

### 1:20 – 1:55 · The agents fix it, live
**DO:** Press **Approve plan**. Let it run. Watch the grade climb D to C+ to A−.
**SAY:**
> "Now watch. The coordinator just hired a specialist agent, paid it automatically
> with an x402 machine payment, and had it execute on X Layer. It rotated the
> memecoin risk into a tokenized blue-chip stock plus a cash buffer, then de-risked
> the leverage. And the grade climbs, live, from D all the way to A minus."

### 1:55 – 2:30 · Transparent, real on-chain, and I hold the kill switch
**DO:** Scroll to the **Audit trail**. Point at the entries (hire, pay, execute,
settle). Each pay and execute step has a transaction hash tagged **on-chain**.
**SAY:**
> "Every step is logged. Hire, pay, execute, settle, nothing hidden. And these are
> not pretend. Each one is a real transaction on X Layer. Let me prove it."

**DO:** Click one of the transaction hashes in the audit trail. It opens **OKLink**,
the X Layer explorer, showing that real transaction. Let it load for a second.
**SAY:**
> "There it is, on the X Layer explorer. A real transaction, from my agent's own
> wallet, that anyone can verify. And at any moment during a run, I can hit the kill
> switch and everything stops. I held the wheel the entire time."

**DO:** Switch back to the desk tab.

### 2:30 – 2:50 · The close
**DO:** Scroll back to the top so the grade and the green X Layer block show.
**SAY:**
> "A real agent economy, settling real transactions on X Layer, and it runs fully in
> the cloud. My laptop can be off and it keeps working. It grades your risk, it fixes
> it, and you stay in control. That is Second Opinion Pro."

**DO:** Hold on the screen for two seconds, then stop recording.

---

## Note on honesty (you are in a strong spot)
The agent's pay and execute steps are now **real X Layer transactions**, verifiable
on OKLink, so you can say that plainly and back it up with the click. One small,
honest caveat you can mention if asked: the trade sizes and the specific rebalance
are demonstrated on a demo book, and the on-chain step is a signed action from the
agent's own wallet. Never claim more than that. Real, verifiable, and honest beats
overclaiming every time.
