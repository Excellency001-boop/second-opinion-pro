# OKX Dev Day 2026 — the hackathon, in detail

Context for the team, so everyone knows what we are building toward and how it is
judged. Sourced from OKX's official Luma page and builder kit.

## What it is
A developer hackathon run by OKX, theme "Build a Market. Build a Company." You
build with **X Layer** (OKX's chain) and **OKX AI**. It starts online and ends with
a live finale in Singapore. OKX says it plainly: **they want working products, not
pitch decks.**

- **Prize:** up to **US$100,000** across winning teams.
- **Our project:** Second Opinion Pro. Primary track OKX AI, also uses X Layer.
- **Our route:** Remote (judged and can win without travelling).

## The two tracks (pick one primary; may use both)
1. **X Layer: tokenized stocks and RWA.** Apps and infrastructure that use tokenized
   stocks or real-world assets on X Layer. Examples: data and portfolio tools,
   asset-based payments or commerce, investor or issuer tooling.
2. **OKX AI: agents and AI-native businesses.** Agent services and AI-native products
   for discovery, coordination, collaboration and monetisation. Examples: agent
   marketplaces, data or API services, tools that help agents transact.

Our build sits in OKX AI (a coordinator agent hiring and paying specialist agents)
and reaches into X Layer (tokenized stocks used in the rebalance).

## Two ways to compete
- **In-person Singapore finale (7 Oct):** teams demo live for the headline awards.
  The largest share of the pool sits here. Travel is self-funded.
- **Remote:** build and submit, still judged, can still win. This is our route.

## What we must submit (one package, via the form)
- Team and track: team name, members, project name, primary track, participation route.
- Project summary: the product, the intended user, the core integration.
- Repository: a public repo with a clear README.
- Demo video: **2 to 4 minutes** showing the working product and the integration.
- Product link: the live deployment.
- Declaration: confirm the information is accurate and accept the guidelines.

Where we stand: repo, live link, and written summary are ready. The demo video is
the last item to record.

## How it is judged (what OKX emphasises)
OKX validates every submission and its links before choosing finalists. Across their
materials the emphasis is consistent:
- A **working product**, not a deck or a mock.
- **Real integration** with X Layer and/or OKX AI.
- A **clear problem and real user value**.
- A **convincing demo** of the product actually doing useful work.
- Technical quality and a production-ready feel.

Note: OKX's builder kit has a precise "how projects will be assessed" section. Treat
the list above as the emphasis and confirm the exact scoring wording from the kit
before we finalise the submission.

## What this means for QA
We are judged remotely on a **working product + public repo + a 2 to 4 minute demo
video**. So the bar is: the product must run flawlessly through the demo flow, look
clean, be understandable to a newcomer in seconds, and make the X Layer + agent
integration visibly and honestly clear. That is exactly what the QA brief tests.
